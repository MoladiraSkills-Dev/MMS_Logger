Employee Shift & Resource Guide

Attendance policies, shift floor times, leave & devices

⏱️ Shift Hours & Floor Anchors

  • Standard Weekdays (Mon - Fri): 08:00 AM – 17:00 PM (9 hours total).
  • Saturdays: 08:00 AM – 13:00 PM (5 hours total).
  • Early Clock-In Floor: Clocking in before 08:00 AM is anchored to 08:00 AM.
  • 5-Minute Rounding: Clocking in after 08:00 AM is dynamically rounded to the nearest 5 minutes (e.g. 08:07 → 08:05, 08:08 → 08:10).
  • Automatic 1-Hour Lunch: Weekday shifts longer than 5 hours automatically deduct a 1-hour lunch break in accordance with the BCEA. Saturdays have zero lunch deduction.
  • Standard Overtime Lock: Overtime is locked to 0 for standard shifts. Any approved overtime or early departures are audited and verified by an Administrator.

📍 GPS Location Telemetry

  • Enable GPS: When prompted by your browser, always select "Allow" for location permissions.
  • Reverse Geocoding: Your coordinates are translated into a verified physical street address in the company activity audit trail.
  • Clock-In First Rule: You cannot clock out in the afternoon unless you have clocked in for today.
  • Single Shift Entry: You can only clock in once and clock out once per day.

📅 Leave & 💻 Hardware Checkouts

  • Leave Applications: Click "Apply for Leave" to submit Sick (SL), Annual (AL), or Family Responsibility (FL) requests. Track approval status in the portal.
  • Device Checkouts: Request field laptops, LTE routers, and scanners. Return hardware promptly at the end of assignments.
